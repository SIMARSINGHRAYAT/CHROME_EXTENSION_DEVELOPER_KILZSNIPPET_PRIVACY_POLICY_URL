# CLV Media Player Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 26, 2026

KILZSNIPPET ("we," "us," or "our") provides CLV Media Player (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application handles when you use it.

## Information We Collect

CLV Media Player does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information with us. The Application does not include advertising or analytics.

## Files and Media

The Application may access files and folders that you explicitly open, drag into the Application, or select through your device's file picker. These files are processed locally on your device for playback or display. CLV Media Player does not upload, transmit, or store copies of your local media files on our servers.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights and permissions required to access and play the media you select.

## Information Stored on Your Device

To provide playback features, the Application may store the following information locally on your device:

- Application preferences, such as playback, audio, video, subtitle, and interface settings.
- A limited list of recently opened media items.
- Playback positions used to resume media.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear recent media and playback information through the Application or by clearing its local application data.

## Network Connections and Third-Party Services

CLV Media Player is designed for local media playback. Some demonstration content or media URLs may be hosted by third parties. If you choose to open online media, your device connects directly to the relevant host, which may process information such as your IP address under that host's privacy policy.

The Application may also request fonts from Google Fonts when an internet connection is available. Google may process technical information associated with that request. For more information, see Google's privacy policy at <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

## Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Security

Because application preferences and playback information are stored locally, you should protect access to your device and user account. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be made available with the Application and will include a revised effective date.
