# Privacy Policy - Live 4K HD Screen Recorder

**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Live 4K HD Screen Recorder ("App", "We", "Our", or "Us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our desktop screen recording application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Recording Data
- **Screen Content:** When you use the screen recording feature, the visual content displayed on your screen is captured locally on your device. This data is NOT transmitted to our servers.
- **Audio:** When recording audio (system audio, microphone, or both), the audio stream is captured locally on your device and NOT transmitted to our servers.
- **Camera Feed:** When using the webcam recording feature, the video stream is captured locally on your device and NOT transmitted to our servers.

### 2.2 Device Permissions
To function properly, the App requests the following permissions:
- **Display/Screen Capture:** Required to record your screen
- **Microphone:** Required to record audio from your microphone
- **Camera:** Required to capture video from your webcam
- **File System:** Required to save recorded files to your device

### 2.3 Technical Information
- **Application Logs:** We may collect error logs and crash reports to improve application stability and performance
- **Browser Information:** Standard browser information (User-Agent, language preferences)
- **Usage Statistics:** Non-personal usage data such as which recording modes are used (screen, camera, audio preferences)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Usage
- To provide the core screen recording functionality
- To encode and save recordings in your desired format (MP4, WebM)
- To display real-time audio level meters during microphone testing
- To optimize video quality based on your selected resolution (240p to 4K)

### 3.2 Secondary Usage
- To improve App performance and user experience
- To identify and fix technical issues and bugs
- To analyze usage patterns (anonymized and non-personal)
- To provide technical support when needed

### 3.3 What We Do NOT Use Your Data For
- We do NOT use recorded content for any commercial purpose
- We do NOT analyze the content of your recordings
- We do NOT share your recordings with third parties
- We do NOT use recordings for training AI models
- We do NOT display or access your recordings without explicit permission

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

### 4.1 Recording Files
- **Local Storage Only:** All recordings are stored locally on your device
- **User Control:** You have full control over your recordings and can delete them at any time
- **No Cloud Backup:** By default, recordings are NOT automatically uploaded to cloud services
- **File Format:** Recordings are saved as MP4 or WebM files on your device

### 4.2 Temporary Files
- **Conversion Cache:** During WebM-to-MP4 conversion, temporary files are created locally and deleted after conversion completes
- **Cache Cleanup:** You can manually clear browser cache at any time

### 4.3 Application Data
- **Settings:** Your recording preferences (quality, format, audio mode) may be stored locally in browser storage
- **Retention:** Settings are retained until you clear browser data or uninstall the App

---

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
- **Local Processing:** All recording and processing happens locally on your device—no data transmission over the internet during recording
- **Encryption in Transit:** If any telemetry data is sent, it uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Signed Packages:** The App is distributed as signed APPX/MSIX packages ensuring integrity and authenticity
- **No Backdoors:** The App contains no remote access, spyware, or malware

### 5.2 Your Responsibilities
- Keep your Windows operating system updated with latest security patches
- Use strong passwords to protect your device
- Be cautious about sharing recorded files
- Regularly back up important recordings

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

### 6.1 FFmpeg WASM
- The App uses FFmpeg WebAssembly (WASM) for video codec conversion
- FFmpeg processes occur locally on your device
- No video data is sent to external servers

### 6.2 Browser APIs
- The App uses standard browser APIs (MediaRecorder, getDisplayMedia, getUserMedia)
- These APIs enforce operating system-level permissions
- Windows controls what access the App has to your screen, camera, and microphone

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 User Control
- **Recording Control:** You have complete control over when to start/stop recording
- **File Deletion:** You can delete any recordings at any time
- **Settings Reset:** You can clear all stored settings and preferences
- **Permission Revocation:** You can revoke App permissions through Windows Settings

### 7.2 Data Access
- You have the right to know what data the App has collected
- You can access all your recordings stored on your device
- You can export or delete your recordings at any time

### 7.3 Data Deletion
- Delete individual recordings directly from your file system
- Clear application cache and storage through browser settings
- Uninstall the App completely to remove all associated data
