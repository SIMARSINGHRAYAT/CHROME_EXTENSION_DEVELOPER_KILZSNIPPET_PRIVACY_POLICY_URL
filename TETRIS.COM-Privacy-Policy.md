# TETRIS.COM Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 24, 2026

TETRIS.COM ("TETRIS.COM", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the TETRIS.COM application.

## 1. Information We Collect

TETRIS.COM is designed to provide its core gameplay experience without requiring users to provide personal information.

We do **not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information** such as:

- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Home address
- Passwords
- Contacts
- Photos or personal files
- Precise location
- Payment information
- Government identification
- Biometric information

TETRIS.COM does not require you to create an account to play the game.

## 2. Information Stored Locally

The application may store game-related information locally on your Windows device, such as:

- Game settings
- Preferences
- Local scores or high scores
- Gameplay configuration
- Application state required for normal operation

This information is used only to provide and maintain the application's functionality.

TETRIS.COM may operate without an internet connection for its core gameplay functionality.

If a future version introduces online functionality, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe any additional data processing associated with those features.

## 4. Advertising

TETRIS.COM does not intentionally collect personal information for targeted advertising.

If advertising or third-party advertising services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

## 5. Payments

The current version of TETRIS.COM does not require users to provide payment information directly to the application.

## 6. Analytics and Tracking

TETRIS.COM does not intentionally use personal tracking technologies to identify individual users.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The current version of TETRIS.COM is not intended to sell or share users' personal information with third parties.

Third-party services may be used in future versions for purposes such as application distribution, crash reporting, analytics, online services, or other functionality. Where such services process information, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 8. Children's Privacy

TETRIS.COM does not intentionally collect personal information from children or require users to provide personal information to use the core game.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information has been submitted to us may contact us using the support information associated with the application.

TETRIS.COM does not maintain personal information for ordinary gameplay.
