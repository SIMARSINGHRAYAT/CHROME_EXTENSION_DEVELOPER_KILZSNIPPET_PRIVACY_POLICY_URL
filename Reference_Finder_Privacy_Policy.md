# Privacy Policy for Reference Finder

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026

Reference Finder is a desktop application that helps users discover
scholarly publications from publicly available academic sources. We are
committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

Reference Finder does **not** collect, store, or sell personal
information.

The application does not require user accounts and does not
intentionally collect information such as names, email addresses, phone
numbers, payment information, or other personally identifiable
information.

## Search Requests

When you perform a search, the search terms you enter (such as keywords,
paper titles, author names, or publication years) are used only to
retrieve relevant scholarly publications from publicly available
academic sources or their official APIs.

Search queries are not retained by the developer unless you explicitly
choose to save them locally.

## Local Storage

If enabled, the application may store the following information
**locally on your device**:

-   Saved bookmarks
-   User preferences
-   Search history
-   Application settings

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the
developer.

## Third-Party Services

Reference Finder may access publicly available scholarly services to
retrieve publication metadata. These services may include providers such
as Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC, ORCID, and official
publisher websites.

Reference Finder is an independent application and is **not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** Google, Elsevier, Springer Nature,
IEEE, ACM, ORCID, or any other publisher or academic organization.

## Data Sharing

Reference Finder does not sell, rent, or share personal information with
third parties.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to help protect locally stored
application data.

## Children's Privacy

Reference Finder is intended for academic and research purposes and is
not specifically directed toward children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will
be reflected by revising the Effective Date.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the
developer using the support information provided in the Microsoft Store
listing.
