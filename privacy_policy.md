# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 27, 2026

KILZSNIPPET ("we", "us", or "our") operates **Blockfall Arcade** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles.

## Information We Collect

Blockfall Arcade does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contact information, payment information, precise location, or other personal information.

The App does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, cookies, or a remote game server.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device using browser local storage:

- High scores, levels, cleared lines, game mode, and the date recorded
- Sound preference, sound volume, and ghost-piece display preference

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to KILZSNIPPET. Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may remove this information.

## Audio

The App uses your device's Web Audio API to generate gameplay sound effects locally. Audio is not recorded, saved, or transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

The App loads fonts from Google Fonts at `fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`. When the App requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser or device information. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's privacy policy:

<https://policies.google.com/privacy>

The App does not send gameplay data or locally stored scores to Google Fonts.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information, no age-based registration is required.

## Data Security

Information stored locally by the App is protected by the operating system and application storage controls. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but the App does not transmit the locally stored game information to us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's data practices change. The updated version will include a new effective date and will be made available with the App or its Microsoft Store listing.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the publisher, **KILZSNIPPET**, through the Blockfall Arcade Microsoft Store listing.
