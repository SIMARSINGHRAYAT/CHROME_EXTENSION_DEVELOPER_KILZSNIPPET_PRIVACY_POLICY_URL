# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 11, 2026

## Pac Maze Privacy Policy

Thank you for using **Pac Maze**.

Pac Maze is designed to respect your privacy. This extension operates entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## Information We Collect

Pac Maze **does not collect** any of the following:

* Personal information
* Names or email addresses
* Browser history
* Website content
* Search history
* Location data
* Contacts
* Financial or payment information
* Authentication credentials
* Device identifiers
* Analytics or usage statistics
* Crash reports
* Cookies
* Any other personally identifiable information

## Local Storage

Pac Maze uses the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely to save game-related information, including:

* High score
* Optional game preferences (if available in future versions)

This information is stored **only on your device** and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Internet Connectivity

Pac Maze does not require an internet connection to function.

The extension does not communicate with external servers or cloud services during normal operation.

## Third-Party Services

Pac Maze does not use:

* Analytics services
* Advertising networks
* Tracking technologies
* Remote code
* External JavaScript libraries
* Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)
* Third-party APIs

All code and assets are packaged within the extension.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose any user data because the extension does not collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

Pac Maze does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect future improvements to the extension. Any updates will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or contact the developer through the extension's official support page.

---

**Summary**

* No personal data is collected.
* No browsing data is collected.
* No tracking or analytics are used.
* No advertisements are displayed.
* No remote code is executed.
* No information leaves your device.
* Only your game high score is stored locally using `chrome.storage.local`.
