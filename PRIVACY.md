# Privacy Policy for VoiceNote AI

**Effective Date:** August 3, 2026

## Introduction

VoiceNote AI ("the Extension") is a browser extension that converts a
user's spoken voice into text and allows users to save, edit, and export
their notes locally. We are committed to protecting your privacy and
being transparent about how the Extension handles your information.

## Information We Collect

By default, VoiceNote AI does **not** collect, store, or transmit
personal information to the developer or any third party.

The Extension processes only the information that is necessary to
perform its single purpose:

-   Audio captured from your microphone after you explicitly grant
    permission.
-   The text transcript generated from your speech.
-   User preferences and settings (such as theme, language, and
    application settings).
-   Saved notes stored locally on your device.

## How Your Data Is Used

Your information is used solely to:

-   Convert speech into text.
-   Display live transcription.
-   Save notes locally.
-   Recover notes after browser restart or accidental closure.
-   Export notes as `.txt` files.
-   Store your preferences.

The Extension does not use your data for advertising, profiling,
analytics, or tracking.

## Data Storage

All notes, settings, and preferences are stored locally using browser
storage technologies such as IndexedDB and Chrome/Edge Storage APIs.

The developer does not have access to your stored data.

## Data Sharing

VoiceNote AI does not sell, rent, or share your personal information
with third parties.

No user data is transferred outside your device unless you explicitly
enable an optional third-party cloud speech recognition provider in a
future version.

## Permissions

### Microphone

Microphone access is requested only after you choose to start recording.
It is used exclusively for speech-to-text transcription.

### Storage

Storage permission is required to save notes, settings, autosave
information, and recovery data locally.

### Offscreen

The offscreen permission is used to maintain reliable speech recognition
during long recording sessions and when the extension popup is not
visible.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are implemented to protect locally stored
information from unauthorized access. However, no software can guarantee
absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

VoiceNote AI is not intended for children under the age required by
applicable law. The Extension does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will
be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the
developer using the contact information provided on the extension's
store listing or project repository.
