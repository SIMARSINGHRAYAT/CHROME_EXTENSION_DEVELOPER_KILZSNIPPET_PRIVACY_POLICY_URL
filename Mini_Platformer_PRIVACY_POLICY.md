# Mini Platformer Privacy Policy

Thank you for using **Mini Platformer**.

Mini Platformer is an offline browser extension game designed with user privacy in mind. The extension operates entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## Information We Collect

Mini Platformer **does not collect**:

* Personal information
* Names
* Email addresses
* Phone numbers
* Browser history
* Website content
* Search history
* Location data
* Contacts
* Financial information
* Authentication credentials
* Device identifiers
* Advertising identifiers
* Analytics or usage statistics
* Crash reports
* Cookies
* Any personally identifiable information

## Local Storage

Mini Platformer may use the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) to save game-related information, including:

* High score
* Game progress
* Optional game preferences or settings

This information is stored **only on your device** and is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Internet Connectivity

Mini Platformer does not require an internet connection to play.

The extension performs all gameplay locally and does not communicate with external servers during normal operation.

## Third-Party Services

Mini Platformer does **not** use:

* Analytics services
* Advertising networks
* User tracking technologies
* Social media integrations
* External APIs
* Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)
* Remote JavaScript
* Remote WebAssembly
* Remote assets

All application code and game resources are packaged within the extension.

## Data Sharing

Because Mini Platformer does not collect user information, no personal data is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Mini Platformer is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

## Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there are no online user accounts or databases associated with this extension. All saved game information remains on the user's local device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the extension evolves. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

## Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the project's official GitHub repository or the support page listed on the browser extension store.
