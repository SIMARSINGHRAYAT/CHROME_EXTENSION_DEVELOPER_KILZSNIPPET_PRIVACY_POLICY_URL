# CLV.COM Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026\
**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026

CLV.COM ("CLV.COM", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This
Privacy Policy explains how CLV.COM handles information when you use the
CLV.COM Windows application (the "App").

CLV.COM is designed as a privacy-focused multimedia application for
viewing and playing photos and videos on your Windows device.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

CLV.COM does not require you to create an account, provide your name,
email address, phone number, postal address, or other personal
information to use the App.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through the App.

### 1.2 Photos, Videos, and Other Local Files

CLV.COM may access photos, videos, and other media files on your device
when you choose to open, import, browse, or play them.

These files are processed for the purpose of providing the App's
multimedia functionality. CLV.COM does not upload, sell, or share your
personal media files with us or with third parties unless a future
feature explicitly provides such functionality and you are informed and,
where required, provide consent.

Your local media remains under your control.

### 1.3 Usage, Analytics, and Tracking

CLV.COM does not intentionally use advertising trackers, behavioral
tracking, or third-party analytics services to build a profile of your
activity.

Unless explicitly stated in a future version of this Privacy Policy,
CLV.COM does not collect information about the videos or photos you view
for advertising or profiling purposes.

### 1.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Windows application does not require cookies for its core
functionality.

If future versions of CLV.COM use cookies or similar technologies for
optional online services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain
their purpose and any applicable controls.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Because CLV.COM is designed to operate primarily on your device,
information and files available to the App are used primarily to:

-   Open and display photos and videos.
-   Play supported media files.
-   Provide playback controls and multimedia features.
-   Remember application settings or preferences locally, where
    applicable.
-   Diagnose technical problems when information is voluntarily provided
    by you for support.

We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising.

## 3. Data Storage

CLV.COM is designed to process local media on your Windows device.

Application settings, preferences, cache data, thumbnails, or other
locally generated data may be stored on your device to provide or
improve functionality. Such data is controlled by the Windows operating
system and the App's local storage mechanisms.

CLV.COM does not intentionally store your personal media on our servers.

If Windows or another operating-system service backs up application data
through a feature such as OneDrive, that processing is governed by the
applicable Microsoft service and its privacy terms.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We do not intentionally share your personal information or local media
files with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for
advertising or profiling.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal
process, court order, or governmental request, or when necessary to
protect the rights, security, and safety of CLV.COM, our users, or
others.

## 5. Third-Party Services

CLV.COM may rely on operating-system components, libraries, codecs, or
other technologies required to provide multimedia functionality.

Where a third-party service is used, that service may process
information according to its own privacy policy and terms. CLV.COM does
not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

If future versions of CLV.COM introduce online services, analytics,
advertising, cloud storage, account functionality, or other third-party
integrations that materially affect privacy, this Privacy Policy will be
updated before or when those features are introduced, as appropriate.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by CLV.COM.

Because CLV.COM is primarily designed to process media locally, the
security of your device, Windows account, file permissions, storage, and
other system components also plays an important role in protecting your
information.

You should keep Windows and CLV.COM updated and use appropriate device
security measures.

## 7. Your Choices and Controls

You control which files and folders you make available to CLV.COM.

You can:

-   Choose which media files to open or play.
-   Remove or move files using Windows.
-   Uninstall CLV.COM at any time.
-   Manage Windows privacy and application permissions through Windows
    Settings, where applicable.
-   Contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

## 8. Children's Privacy

CLV.COM is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children
through the App. Because CLV.COM does not require an account or
intentionally collect personal information for its core functionality,
children can use the App without providing personal information to
CLV.COM.

Parents or guardians who have questions about privacy and a child's use
of CLV.COM may contact us using the information provided below.

## 9. International Use

CLV.COM may be made available to users in different countries.

If information is processed outside your country, such processing will
be carried out in accordance with applicable privacy laws and this
Privacy Policy.
