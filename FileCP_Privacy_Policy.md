# Privacy Policy for FileCP

**Effective Date:** July 18, 2026

## Introduction

FileCP ("the Extension") is designed to provide secure, temporary file
sharing between devices. We value your privacy and are committed to
protecting your information.

## Information We Collect

FileCP is designed to minimize data collection.

Unless explicitly required for a transfer session, the Extension does
**not** collect, store, sell, or share personal information such as:

-   Name
-   Email address
-   Phone number
-   Payment information
-   Browsing history
-   Location data

## File Transfers

Files shared through FileCP are transferred using temporary sessions.
Transfer sessions automatically expire after a short period. FileCP is
designed so that transferred files are not retained longer than
necessary to complete the transfer.

## How Information Is Used

Any information required to establish a temporary transfer session is
used solely to:

-   Create and manage a secure transfer session.
-   Deliver files between participating devices.
-   Maintain the functionality and security of the Extension.

No information is used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

## Third-Party Services

FileCP does not sell or share user data with third parties for
advertising purposes.

If backend infrastructure is used solely to facilitate file transfers,
it is used only for providing the requested service.

## Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to help
protect data during transfer. While no method of electronic transmission
is completely secure, FileCP is designed with privacy and security as
core principles.

## Children's Privacy

FileCP is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will
be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this document.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the
developer through the official FileCP project or support channel.
