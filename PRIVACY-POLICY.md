# LUMIVERSE Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 8, 2026

LUMIVERSE is a desktop multimedia application for organizing and playing local
media and opening third-party media and streaming websites. This policy
describes information the application may handle and the choices available to
you.

## Information LUMIVERSE handles

Depending on the features you use and the application’s configuration,
LUMIVERSE may handle:

- **Media library details:** media titles, file names, file paths or URLs, media
  types, formats, and available attributes such as duration, dimensions, and
  file size. Local media is selected and played by you in the application.
- **Playlists:** playlist names, descriptions, artwork references, track
  titles, artist and album labels, track order, and media file paths or URLs.
- **Favorites and recent activity:** items you mark as favorites and records of
  recently accessed media or destinations. Recent records may include an item
  title, media type, URL or path, playback progress, duration, and access time.
- **Application preferences:** settings you choose, such as appearance,
  playback, and subtitle preferences.
- **Information processed by third-party websites:** when you open a streaming
  service or external website, that provider may receive information through
  your connection and any information you submit to it.

LUMIVERSE does not provide an account-registration feature in the application.
Do not treat this statement as a description of accounts you may have with
third-party services.

## How information is used

The application uses the information above to display your media library,
manage playlists and favorites, show recent activity, remember preferences,
play media, and open the destinations you select.

LUMIVERSE can check the availability of its configured external sources. These
checks make network requests to the source websites and report their
availability in the application. When default playlist artwork is displayed,
the application may request that image from its configured image host.

## Storage and service configuration

LUMIVERSE’s data-backed features use a PostgreSQL service configured by the
application operator. When that service is enabled, the application sends the
relevant library, playlist, favorite, recent-activity, and settings requests to
that service. The service operator is responsible for its hosting, access
controls, backups, retention, and deletion practices. If no database service is
configured, database-backed features may not work.

Information in the database is retained according to the service operator’s
retention practices and until it is deleted by you using available application
controls or by the service operator. Removing an item in the application may
not remove copies retained in service backups.

When you use third-party websites in the application, those sites may store
cookies or other site data in the application’s browser session. Their
collection and use of information are governed by their own privacy policies
and settings, not this policy.

## Sharing and third-party services

LUMIVERSE does not control third-party streaming platforms, media websites, or
the configured database service. Those services may collect information such
as your IP address, browser or device details, and information you provide
directly to them. Review the privacy policies of any services you choose to
use.

The application’s external-source availability checks contact the relevant
websites directly. Those websites may receive ordinary connection information
and may log the requests. LUMIVERSE does not determine their logging or
retention practices.

## Your choices

You can choose which local media to open and which third-party destinations to
visit. Available application controls let you manage playlists and favorites,
clear recent activity, and reset certain preferences. You can also contact the
operator of the configured database service to ask about data access or
deletion. The operator may need to verify your request and comply with
applicable legal requirements.

You can manage cookies and site data through the application or Windows
controls available for the installed app. Clearing site data may sign you out
of third-party websites or reset their preferences.

## Security

The application and its configured service should be kept up to date and
operated with appropriate safeguards. No application, network transmission, or
storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Contact the service
operator if you believe information associated with the service has been
accessed improperly.
