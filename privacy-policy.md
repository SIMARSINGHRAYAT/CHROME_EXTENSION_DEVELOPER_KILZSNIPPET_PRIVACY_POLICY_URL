## Information handled by the App

The App displays `https://meowtv.ru/` using Microsoft Edge WebView2. The App's
source code does not implement an account system, advertising profile,
analytics, or a telemetry service that sends usage data to the publisher.

When the App connects to the website or its content providers, those services
may receive information such as your IP address, browser and device
characteristics, requested URLs, and information you choose to enter on the
website. The website and its providers may use cookies or similar technologies
and may collect activity information according to their own policies. We do not
control their collection or use of information.

The App enables WebView2 strict tracking prevention and blocks requests to a
built-in list of known advertising domains. It also blocks external pop-up
windows and filters certain promotional overlays. These measures may reduce
some third-party requests, but they do not prevent all data collection by the
website or its providers.

## Local storage

WebView2 may store website data on your device, including cookies, cached
resources, and site storage, to support the website and retain its state between
uses. The App does not provide a separate control for clearing this data. You
can manage or remove application data using Windows settings; removing it may
sign you out of the website or reset its saved state.

## Microsoft and Windows services

The App requires Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. Microsoft may process
technical, diagnostic, or usage information in connection with Windows and
WebView2, subject to Microsoft's settings and privacy terms. Review the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)
and your Windows diagnostic-data settings for details.

## Sharing and sale

The App does not provide the publisher with a data-collection or analytics
backend. Information sent while visiting the website is handled by the website
and any services it uses, not by the App's publisher through an App-operated
service. The App's publisher does not sell personal information collected by
the App.

## Retention and security

Website data held in the WebView2 profile may remain on your device until
removed through Windows or otherwise cleared. Network information handled by the
website, its providers, Microsoft, or Windows is subject to their respective
retention and security practices. We do not control those practices.

## Children's privacy

The App does not include a publisher-operated account or data collection
service. The website may have its own age restrictions and practices. Parents
and guardians should review the website's terms and privacy information before
allowing a child to use it.
